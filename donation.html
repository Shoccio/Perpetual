Sustainable Donations and Funding Policy

Overview:

The Community Outreach Department (COD) of the University of Perpetual Help System Laguna (UPHSL) is an office responsible for the implementation of community outreach and extension programs of the university. Its main objectives include, but are not limited to, improvement of health status of a community, enhancement of literacy of the community members, and cultivation of values and culture toward attaining improved quality of life as we exercise our virtue that Perpetualites are helpers of God. Thus, this sustainable donation and fundings policy presented our assurance and commitment in accepting donations, funds, and sponsorships which sustains our mission of building a better nation through our flagship programs in health and wellness, literacy, environmental, livelihood, disaster response, and values formation.

Objectives

The UPHSL and COD together with its partners are encouraging Sustainable Donations and Fundings:

  1. To continue our mission of helping our adopted communities through community outreach programs.
  2. To integrate healthy social and environmental initiatives as part of accepting the donations and funds.
  3. To collaborate with local, national, and international government and non- government organizations to maximize the donations and funds for the benefit of wider community.
  4. To fortify the sustainable donations and funds through a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) upon acceptance of donations, funds, and sponsorships.

Guiding Principles

The UPHSL and COD together with its partners set the following Principles for Sustainable Donations and Fundings:

  1. Donations, fundings, and sponsorships must support the mission and objectives of UPHSL and the COD.
  2. Donations, fundings, and sponsorships must consider and support the 17 Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations.
  3. Donations, fundings, and sponsorships must maintain transparency through an approved concept paper and deed of donations to ensure proper usage and accountability in using the funds.
  4. Donations and fundings must adhere ethical consideration and legal requirements specially in accepting donations, fundings, and sponsorships to avoid conflicts and maintain integrity of the university.
  5. In accepting donations, fundings, and sponsorships, the donors, funding partners, and stakeholders must build relationships based on mutual respect, trust, and shared values, fostering collaboration and partnership in advancing our mission.

Donations/Funding Criteria:

The UPHSL and COD together with its partners established the following Criteria for Sustainable Donations and Fundings:

  1. Donations and funding must align with the university and COD's mission, objectives, core values, and strategic priorities, supporting initiatives that drive innovation, excellence, and sustainability.
  2. Donations and funding that demonstrate a positive impact on environmental sustainability, social equity, and ethical governance, contributing to a more sustainable and inclusive future.
  3. The university will ensure that acceptance of donations does not compromise the academic freedom, independence, or integrity as an institution.
  4. The university will seek donations and funding from partners who are committed to long-term collaboration, mutual learning, and shared progress towards our sustainability goals.

Implementation

The UPHSL and COD together with its partners considered the following prior to the implementation for Sustainable Donations and Fundings:

  1. Engage with potential donors and funding partners to communicate the partnership opportunities, funding and sponsorship needs, program priorities, and impact of the program/project.
  2. Maintain transparency in all fundraising activities and sharing of resources through accomplishment reports and fund utilization report.
  3. Establish ethical considerations for donations and funding acceptance, including review by the ethical board and other governing bodies of the university to ensure its relevance, accuracy, and proper utilization following certain policies and guidelines.

Review and Revision

  • This Sustainable Donations and Funding Policy of the university will be reviewed and revised to ensure its effectiveness, relevance, and alignment with the institutional vision and sustainability goals annually.
  • The revisions or enhancements to the policy will be guided by feedback from the university's stakeholders, industry partners, and adopted community.
