POLICY ON ACADEMIC FREEDOM

1. Purpose

The UNIVERSITY OF PERPETUAL HELP SYSTEM affirms its commitment in upholding and protecting the principle of academic freedom, ensuring that faculty, students, and staff can engage in intellectual inquiry, scholarship, and teaching without undue interference or restrictions. This policy is essential for fostering an environment that promotes free expression, critical thinking, and the pursuit of knowledge.

2. Definition of Academic Freedom

Academic freedom entails the rights of educational institutions, faculty, and students in higher education to determine for themselves on academic grounds who may teach, what may be taught, how it shall be taught, and who may be admitted to study. This includes:

  • Teaching: Freedom to teach and discuss all relevant matters in the classroom.
  • Research: Freedom to explore any area of scholarship and to publish findings.
  • Expression: Freedom to express one's opinions as citizens without institutional censorship or discipline.
  • Curricula: The autonomy to determine the curricula and academic programs offered.

3. Legal Framework

The 1987 Philippine Constitution guarantees the academic freedom within the UPHSL community which states, "Academic freedom shall be enjoyed in all institutions of higher learning." Hence, this policy aligns with this constitutional provision and relevant jurisprudence to protect the exercise of academic freedom across educational endeavors.

4. Rights and Responsibilities

4.1 Rights of Academic Staff

  • Freedom to Teach: Faculty members are entitled to teach their subjects with appropriate independence, selecting materials and methods consistent with established academic standards.
  • Research Freedom: Faculty have the liberty to conduct research, publish results, and disseminate knowledge without external constraints, provided that it adheres to ethical standards.
  • Freedom of Speech: Faculty members may express their scholarly views in public forums without fear of institutional reprimand.

4.2 Rights of Students

  • Engagement in Inquiry: Students are entitled to engage in discussions and activities that enhance their academic journeys without fear of retribution for expressing their views.
  • Participatory Rights: Students have the right to be involved in the development of academic programs and policies affecting their education.

4.3 Responsibilities

  • Intellectual Honesty: All members must maintain intellectual honesty and uphold ethical conduct in teaching and research.
  • Respectful Discourse: Faculty and students are expected to engage in respectful discourse and recognize the perspectives of others, fostering a dynamic learning environment.

5. Institutional Autonomy

The University of Perpetual Help System Laguna recognizes the right to institutional autonomy free from political, religious, and corporate influence. This autonomy allows for the pursuit of knowledge to be conducted progressively while adhering to the institution’s educational mission.

6. Process to Address Violations

Complaints regarding perceived violations of academic freedom shall be addressed through established institutional grievance procedures. Investigations will be conducted impartially, considering the rights of all parties involved. Appropriate actions will be implemented to remedy violations where warranted.

7. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure compliance with evolving standards of academic freedom, maintaining its relevance and effectiveness in promoting a thriving academic community.
